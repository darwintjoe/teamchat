Three ways this becomes a business

1
Enterprise
Orgs above ~1,000 staff — the ones with budget and compliance needs. Self-hosted, paid, available as soon as they appear. The largest org can never set the cost of the smallest.
2
The embed layer
Any business app that wants in-app chat plus tasks. Accurate is customer one, not the market. Every ERP, POS and field-service tool needs this and none of them should build it.
3
The accountability graph
Who claims, who closes, how fast, who drops. No chat app has this data, because no chat app has a task state. It is the asset that makes layers 1 and 2 defensible.
