What is TeamChat — and why does the world need a fourth one?
WhatsApp remembers what was said.
TeamChat remembers who has to do it.
1

The problem you already have

In a group chat, a request looks exactly like a joke. It scrolls away. Three days later it is still nobody's job — and nobody can prove who dropped it. You cannot search for "who was supposed to do this". There is no such search.
Budi, did you send outlet 7's invoice?
Yes pak, in the group earlier
Which group? I don't see it.
3 days later — still nobody's job
2

One button changes everything

Action  ·  from Head Office
Send outlet 7's invoice before Friday close.
⚡ Claim Task Claimed by Rina
The first person to tap Claim takes the job. Everyone else's button goes grey instantly — like grabbing a ringing phone.

4

Two kinds of message

Action
A job with an owner
Support tickets, assignments, urgent jobs. Someone must claim it.
Discussion
Normal chat
Questions, announcements, opinions. Nobody owns it.
5
